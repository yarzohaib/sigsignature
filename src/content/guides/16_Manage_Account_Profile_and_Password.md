## Overview: Account Administration & Security

Your SigSignature account houses your saved signature configurations, uploaded high-resolution assets, custom team templates, and billing history. Keeping your profile updated ensures accurate team notifications and seamless customer support.

This guide explains how to manage your personal profile, update security credentials, and handle registered email modifications.

---

## Step-by-Step Account Management

### 1. Navigating to Account Settings
1. Log into your dashboard at `sigsignature.com`.
2. In the left navigation sidebar, click on **Settings** (or click your profile avatar in the top right corner -> **Settings**).
3. The Settings screen is divided into two primary tabs: **Profile** and **Security**.

### 2. Updating Profile Information
Under the **Profile Settings** section:
* **Full Name:** Update your legal or preferred display name. This name is used for team workspace invites and account invoicing.
* **Profile Avatar:** Upload a PNG or JPEG photo (up to 2MB). A square 1:1 aspect ratio is recommended. This avatar serves as your user profile icon in the SigSignature dashboard.
* Click **Save Profile Changes**. A confirmation toast will indicate that your details have been updated.

### 3. Updating Your Password
Under the **Password & Security** section:
1. Enter your **Current Password**.
2. Enter your **New Password** (must contain at least 8 characters, including a combination of uppercase letters, numbers, and symbols).
3. Re-enter your new password in **Confirm New Password**.
4. Click **Update Password**.
5. All active browser sessions will be refreshed securely.

## Registered Email Policy & Changes

> [!NOTE]
> **Security Protocol:** To protect your account from unauthorized hijacking and protect team templates, **registered account email addresses cannot be modified directly within the UI settings**.

If your organization has rebranded or your work email has changed:
1. Submit a verification request to our engineering team at `support@thenevon.com`.
2. Include your current registered email, your new target email, and your most recent Stripe invoice number for verification.
3. Our security team will process the email transfer within 24 business hours without any loss of your saved signatures.

---

## Deleting an Account

If you wish to permanently delete your SigSignature account:
* Go to **Settings** -> Scroll to the **Danger Zone** at the bottom.
* Click **Delete Account Permanently**.
* Confirm your password.
* *Note:* Deleting your account will immediately remove all saved signatures and revoke active team workspace seats. However, any email signatures already exported into your email clients will continue to function normally.

## Key Takeaways
