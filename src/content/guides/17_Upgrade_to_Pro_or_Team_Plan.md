## Step-by-Step: Upgrading Your Account

### Step 1: Open Pricing & Billing
1. Log into your account and click **Pricing & Billing** in the left sidebar (or click the blue **Upgrade Now** badge in the bottom-left account widget).
2. Review the plan options. Toggle between **Monthly** and **Annual (Save 25%)** billing.

### Step 2: Select Your Plan
* To unlock unlimited personal signatures: Click **Upgrade to Pro**.
* To manage team members and company templates: Click **Upgrade to Team** and select your required seat count (starting at 5 seats).

### Step 3: Secure Checkout via Stripe
1. You will be redirected to the secure Stripe checkout portal.
2. Enter your credit/debit card details (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) or choose Apple Pay / Google Pay.
3. If purchasing on behalf of a company, enter your company name and **VAT / Tax ID** for tax-exempt invoicing.
4. Click **Subscribe**.

### Step 4: Immediate Feature Activation
Upon payment completion, Stripe instantly redirects you back to your SigSignature dashboard. All plan limits are automatically lifted, and premium templates are unlocked immediately.
