## Overview: Frictionless Subscription Management

SigSignature uses **Stripe** as our global payment infrastructure provider. Stripe handles all payment processing with bank-grade AES-256 encryption. We never store or process your credit card numbers on our local servers.

Through the integrated Stripe Customer Portal, you have complete self-service control over your payment methods, billing history, and tax invoices.

---

## Accessing the Billing Portal

1. Log into your SigSignature dashboard.
2. Click **Pricing & Billing** in the left sidebar.
3. Under the **Current Plan** overview, click **Manage Billing & Invoices**.
4. You will be authenticated securely into the Stripe Customer Portal.

---

## Common Billing Tasks

### 1. Updating Payment Methods (Credit Card)
* Inside the Stripe portal, locate the **Payment Method** section.
* Click **+ Add payment method**.
* Enter your new card details and click **Add**.
* To set it as primary, click the three dots (`...`) next to the card and select **Make default**.
* Remove expired cards to prevent automated subscription interruptions.

### 2. Downloading Official Tax Invoices & Receipts
* Scroll down to the **Billing History / Invoices** section of the portal.
* You will see a chronological list of every charge date, amount, and payment status.
* Click on any transaction to download an official **PDF Invoice** or **Receipt**.
* Invoices include transaction timestamps, line-item breakdowns, and tax rates suitable for corporate bookkeeping.

### 3. Adding Your Corporate Tax ID / VAT Number
If your company requires VAT, GST, or Tax IDs on invoices:
1. In the Stripe Customer Portal, click **Update information** under *Billing Information*.
2. Enter your official company legal entity name, corporate address, and Tax ID (e.g., `GB123456789`).
3. Click **Save**. All past and future invoices will automatically regenerate with your tax credentials.

### 4. Canceling a Subscription
We believe canceling should be as simple as subscribing:
1. In the Stripe Customer Portal, click **Cancel plan**.
2. Confirm your cancellation.
3. **What Happens Next:** Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period (no prorated surprise charges). You retain full Pro/Team features until that date.
4. **Permanent Guarantee:** Even after your plan reverts to Free, **all signatures you have already exported and installed into Gmail or Outlook will continue to function permanently**. We will never disable or break your existing email signatures.
