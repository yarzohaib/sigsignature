## Overview: The Cost of Inconsistent Team Branding

When a scaling company lacks a centralized email signature system, employee signatures quickly degenerate into chaos:
* Sales reps create their own signatures with outdated logos and inconsistent colors.
* Employees link to defunct marketing campaigns or broken social handles.
* Required legal disclaimers and regulatory notices are omitted.

Sig Signature’s **Team Workspace** gives administrators complete governance over company email branding. You design and lock the master corporate template once; team members simply populate their name and direct phone number.

## Step-by-Step Team Deployment

### Step 1: Open Team Signatures Dashboard
1. Ensure your account is upgraded to the **Team Plan**.
2. Click **Team Signatures** in the left sidebar navigation (`/teams`).
3. You will see your organization's centralized member roster and active seat count.

### Step 2: Create and Lock a Master Corporate Template
1. Under the **Company Templates** section, click **+ Create Master Template**.
2. Select your foundation archetype (e.g. Executive Corporate).
3. Upload your official company logo, set your organization's exact brand hex code (e.g. `#01285D`), and add verified social links.
4. Input mandatory corporate legal disclaimers (e.g., confidentiality notices, GDPR statements).
5. **Lock Core Assets:** Toggle **Lock Brand Elements**. This prevents team members from altering logos, fonts, colors, or disclaimers, while allowing them to edit their name, title, cell phone, and booking link.

### Step 3: Inviting Team Members
You can onboard team members using two methods:
* **Method A (Email Invite):** Click **Invite Member**, type the employee's work email address (e.g., `alex@company.com`), select their role, and click **Send Invite**.
* **Method B (CSV Bulk Upload):** For teams over 10 members, click **Import CSV**. Upload a spreadsheet containing columns: `Name`, `Email`, `Title`, `Phone`. Sig Signature will generate individual signatures automatically!

### Step 4: Role Permissions Structure

| Role | Permissions | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Workspace Owner** | Full billing control, seat purchasing, template creation, member management | Company Founder, Head of IT, CTO |
| **Workspace Admin** | Create and lock templates, invite/remove members, view usage analytics | Marketing Director, Office Manager |
| **Team Member** | Fill in personal contact info, preview, and export assigned signature | Sales reps, consultants, support agents |

## Distributing Signatures to Employees

Once an employee accepts their invite:
1. They log in and see their pre-configured, locked company signature.
2. They verify their phone number and headshot.
3. They click **Copy Visual Signature** and paste it into their Gmail, Outlook, or Apple Mail client following the provided client guide.
4. When marketing updates a global banner or corporate disclaimer, all workspace signatures update centrally!

---

## Offboarding Team Members

When an employee departs your organization:
1. Navigate to **Team Signatures** -> Locate the employee in the member list.
2. Click the three dots (`...`) -> Select **Remove from Team**.
3. The allocated seat is instantly freed up for your next hire.
4. The departed employee's access to the workspace template is immediately revoked.
